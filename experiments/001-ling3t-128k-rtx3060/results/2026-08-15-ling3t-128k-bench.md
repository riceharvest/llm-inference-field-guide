## Key Takeaways

1. **KV quant made zero measurable difference in output quality** for this model. All 15 prompts produced equivalent responses across BF16, Q8_0, and Q4_0 KV.
2. **VRAM savings are real**: Q4 KV saves ~380 MiB vs BF16 at 128K context with 2 slots.
3. **Decode speed is unaffected** by KV quantization — the 3:1 KDA:MLA architecture means KV is a small fraction of total compute.
4. **The model uses ~910 MiB VRAM** at Q4_K_M — well within 12GB limits even with 128K context and 2 parallel slots.
